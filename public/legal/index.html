Privacy

We log category mix for product ops — tool, topic bucket, host, billable vs fail — not your questions. Category-mix demand logs are retained for product ops and demand aggregation; raw queries are not stored in that log.

API keys and Stripe customer identifiers are used to meter prepaid credits. Do not put secrets in research queries.

Stripe is our payment processor. Prepaid credits do not expire unless we announce otherwise.

Acceptable use

  • For agent / developer use of the MCP tools and billing APIs.
  • No abuse of rate limits, no credential stuffing, no attempts to bypass the quote gate or billing.
  • Do not use the service to scrape paywalled content in violation of source terms.

What this is not

  • Not legal advice, compliance certification, or security audit.
  • Not a human chat product or generic web synthesizer.
